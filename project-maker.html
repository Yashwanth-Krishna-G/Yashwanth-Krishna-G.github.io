Smart Water-Filling System

An automatic, WiFi-controlled water-filling rig with built-in water-quality checking — start, stop and monitor remotely from a phone.

  • Level sensing stops the fill exactly when the vessel is full — no overflow, ever.
  • A quality probe checks the water before the valve opens.
  • An ESP microcontroller exposes the whole rig over WiFi for remote start/stop and monitoring.

3D-Printed Speaker

A custom speaker with a fully 3D-printed enclosure — designed in CAD, printed in PLA and assembled from scratch, driver to terminals.

  • Enclosure volume and port designed for the driver, then printed as a single shell.
  • Internal damping and a tuned bass port for a fuller low end.
  • An exercise in designing for the printer: wall thickness, infill and layer direction all matter acoustically.
