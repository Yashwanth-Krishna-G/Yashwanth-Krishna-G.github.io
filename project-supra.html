Supra SAE India — Powertrain

SolidWorksFEAPowertrain B.Tech Major Project2018 – 2019

As part of Supra SAE India 2019, our team designed and built a formula-style race car to SAE standards. My focus: the powertrain system, from CAD to cost report.

Design & manufacturing

I designed, analysed and fabricated powertrain components including engine mounts and transmission sprockets, and developed detailed CAD models for 40+ components across subsystems — integrating them into the full vehicle assembly in SolidWorks with an eye on weight, manufacturability and vibration stability.

Integration & analysis

The powertrain was integrated into a tube-frame chassis. FEA and dynamic simulations ensured high-stress regions were reinforced while keeping overall mass optimised. The final design met safety and performance criteria, ready for manufacturing and on-track evaluation.

Cost analysis & competition

I prepared a detailed cost-analysis report covering materials, machining and manufacturing feasibility, benchmarking each part against SAE India's budget criteria. The vehicle was successfully presented and competed at the national event — robust design, precision manufacturing and a lot of teamwork.
