03 — About

From CAD & CFD to AI-driven design

I cut my teeth deep in simulation — formula cars, aircraft, lab hardware — and I'm now focused on a single idea: AI tools that don't replace physics, but design with it.

Fig. 02 — On the podium at IIT Guwahati.

The short version

Mechanical engineer; M.Tech in Computational Mechanics from IIT Guwahati. My research builds generative-design frameworks with deep-learning surrogates for structural and thermal–fluid topology optimisation — models that learn what an optimiser would do, then do it in a single forward pass.

Today I design electric machines at SEDEMAC in Pune, bringing the same simulation-first mindset to production hardware.

Fig. 03 — Presenting our bionic arm to my guide.

Hands-on across domains

I've led powertrain design on a Supra SAE car, engineered the fuselage and CFD for an SAE Aero aircraft, and built a 3D-printed compliant bionic arm. In industry and national labs I've worked on underwater robotics, in-space 3D printing and precision flow metering — and I hold a granted patent (No. 589444) for a cable-actuated 3D-printer arm.
